# Projection of R8_research_real_adapters (round 0)

The R8 plan cannot go to the implementer as written: it needs amendments first. The biggest problem: as written, a listing site that refuses the app's download (Etsy answers "forbidden", Shopify stores answer "too many requests") would make the whole research run fail with "key rejected". The plan wants those pages recorded as "could not be read". Several test rows also expect things the recorded provider replies cannot produce: a unit-conversion answer that is off by a factor of ten, a designer name that is not on the captured page, and replies for three model stages that were never captured. The cost mapping also hits a gap in the price units: OpenAI now bills "cache write" tokens, and the contract has no unit for them. One question needs the owner: whether the shop's other web addresses should be readable (card 1). The coordinator handles everything else from the book and the evidence, then compiles the implementer prompt.

## ⚠ OWNER DECISIONS REQUIRED (1)

### Card 1: Should research know your shop's other web addresses?
**Question.** Should the Shopify app get the "read markets" permission so research can see every address your shop answers on (yes / no, primary address only)?

**Story.** Research has to recognise your own shop so that a piece is never used as evidence for itself. Today the app can only read the main address (beyovintage.se) and the internal myshopify address. Say your shop also answers on beyovintage.com or a country address. A search hit there would count as an outside dealer confirming the designer, and the piece's own listing would vouch for itself at "medium" confidence.

**Branches.**
- **Yes:** you add "read markets" to the custom app in Shopify admin and reinstall it. Research then knows every address.
- **No:** research uses the main address plus the myshopify one. That is safe only if the shop has no other address.

**Recommendation.** Say No if beyovintage.se is your only address (every own-listing hit in the benchmark used it). Otherwise say Yes.

**On silence.** The gate holds on this point only. R8 is built to read the two addresses it can see, and "other addresses" stays open, not guessed.
