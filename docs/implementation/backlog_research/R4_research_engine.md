- **R4 reviewer r1 (note → book 08B MC-A6/MC-A7, orchestrator; R7·U1): saving the keys, or switching Automatic back on, queues no run for a session that already has a Main photo and never ran.** `settings_changed` only cancels (implementer assumption 16). MC-A6 says "eligibility is evaluated on … every save of a research setting or key", and 08A §5 says a run is queued when a session "has at least one Main photo and research has not run for it yet". MC-A7 says a setting "never starts" a run. The implementer took the cautious reading, so old sessions are not all researched at once when keys are entered. Why: the photographer enters the keys mid-session and nothing happens until the next capture or **Run again**. The book should say which reading holds (for example: the open session only, or none).
- **R4 reviewer r1 (note → R9·U2, book 14 MA12): a `not_sent` call keeps its recorded request but has no outbound rows.** `gateway.rs` deletes the outbound rows of an offline call (assumption 8): nothing left the Mac, which is MA3's intent. MA12 says literally that "the photos in every recorded provider request are exactly its outbound rows". Why: an acceptance checker written from MA12's wording would flag every offline call. Either the checker skips `not_sent` calls or MA12 says "every sent request".

Follow-ups reuse their reading units, so this mostly bites on a manual **Run again** with a smaller cap. Why: a correct medium suggestion drops to low because money ran out, not because evidence turned against it.
