# Projection of H3_bring_back (round 0)

The bring-back plan can be built, and its core claim holds up in the code: once a photo is brought back, the next Publish leaves it alone unless it was changed. Four test rows need fixing before the build starts. Two of them would pass without checking what they claim to check. One leaves open where a newly shot photo lands in a templated product after a bring back. As written, that photo can jump to first place in Main and become the product's cover picture on the next Publish. One row cannot be set up without a test hook the plan does not name. Two more points are not tests: nothing checks that what the shop sends back is really a JPEG, and the plan never says the stored file path must be in the app's standard form. Nothing needs the owner. The coordinator applies the amendments below, then compiles the implementer prompt.

## ⚠ OWNER DECISIONS REQUIRED (0)

Nothing needs the owner. L3, the template landing, is settled from 10G §4.2/§4.4/§6.4. The coordinator may turn it into a card if it finds that derivation thin.
