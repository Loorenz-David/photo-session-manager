# Projection of H4_remote_workspace (round 0)

The plan can be built, but it is not ready to hand to the implementer yet. The most serious gap is the camera's folder. The plan blocks it from moving to an archived product in one place but misses a second step the app runs first. With the owner's own camera (the direct Sony connection), new photos would be filed into the archived product, and every test the plan asks for would still pass. Two more gaps would cause trouble later: a product whose Shopify check fails would re-ask Shopify over and over while it is open, and the "details only" publish of an archived product would be refused every time because of a check the plan does not mention. None of this needs the owner. The orchestrator can settle all of it from the book and the master plan with paragraph amendments, then compile the implementer prompt.
